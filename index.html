Three woods

Each one ages differently

We work with three species only, so we know every board. Tap one to try it on your table.

European oak

From FSC forests near Biržai and Anykščiai. Strong, heavy, with silver ray flecks you see when the light is low. It turns from straw to a warm honey colour in about two years.

  • HardnessJanka 1,360
  • Ages toHoney
  • From€1,420

Ash

Pale and calm, with a long straight grain. Lighter than oak and slightly springy, so it forgives a dropped pan. With white oil it stays almost Nordic white.

  • HardnessJanka 1,320
  • Ages toSoft cream
  • From€1,290

European walnut

From one sawmill in the Dordogne, cut and dried for three years before it reaches us. Deep brown with purple notes that soften to a mellow, golden brown.

  • HardnessJanka 1,010
  • Ages toGolden brown
  • From€2,090

The workshop

Six people, one table at a time

Nothing is outsourced. The same two hands that choose your boards fit the last joint and oil the top. Swipe the steps, tap a photo to see it larger.

  1. 01 · Week 1

    Dried to 8%

    The timber rests in our kiln and then in the workshop for three weeks, so it settles to the air of a heated home before we cut it.

  2. 02 · Week 3

    Boards matched by eye

    We lay out every board of your top on the floor and move them until the grain flows across. This takes longer than gluing them.

  3. 03 · Week 5

    Joints cut by hand

    Mortise and tenon, wedged and glued. The top floats on the frame on wooden buttons, so it can move with the seasons and never cracks.

  4. 04 · Week 7

    Three coats of oil

    A hard-wax oil from Germany, rubbed in by hand with a day between coats. Water beads on it; a scratch can be sanded out at home.

Questions

Anything else: hello@nendre.example or +370 600 00000.

Does the 3D view match the real table?

The shape, the size and the joinery do, exactly. Wood is a natural material, so the grain on your top will be its own; that is why we send a photo of your boards laid out before we glue them, and you can ask for a change.

How does “See it in your room” work?

On an iPhone or iPad, tap the button and the table opens in Apple's AR view at its real size. Point the camera at the floor and walk round it. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded.

Can I have a size that is not in the list?

Yes. Any length from 120 to 320 cm and any width from 75 to 120 cm costs the same per square metre. Write to us with the size.

Will a solid top crack under floor heating?

No, because the top is never screwed down. It sits on wooden buttons that let it move a few millimetres each season. Keep it about a metre from a radiator, as with any solid wood.

How do I look after it?

Wipe it with a damp cloth. Once a year, rub in a little of the oil from the care kit. Rings and small scratches sand out with the pad in the kit.

Can I see the tables before I order?

Yes. The workshop and a small showroom with eight tables are open on Saturdays from 10 to 15, by appointment. Book a visit below.
